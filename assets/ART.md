# Art provenance

The four `backgrounds/*.png` images were generated specifically for Coquí del Yunque with OpenAI image generation. They are original commissioned-in-session illustrations, not extracted game assets. No Nintendo/Sega art or uploaded ROM data was used.

Shared prompt direction: a polished 2D side-scrolling platform environment, wide 1536 × 1024 landscape, painterly rounded volumes with crisp contours and fine surface detail, saturated inviting color, layered distant scenery, open lower half, no characters/text/HUD/coins/logos.

- `forest.png`: Puerto Rican El Yunque, emerald peaks, tabonuco trees, ferns, vines, waterfall, turquoise river, heliconias and warm light shafts.
- `cavern.png`: limestone karst arches, stalactites, waterfall, turquoise pools, amber sinkhole light and cool navy shadows.
- `mangrove.png`: moonlit bioluminescent bay, red-mangrove stilt roots, electric-blue water, purple clouds and far islands.
- `summit.png`: Sierra de Luquillo above a cloud sea, lavender/peach storm light, green peaks, small palms and a distant observation tower.

These are stylized fantasy environments, not geographically exact depictions. The coquí, enemies, terrain, powers and effects are drawn and animated procedurally in `src/render.js`. Audio is synthesized in `src/audio.js`.
